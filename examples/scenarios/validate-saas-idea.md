# Scenario: Validate a SaaS idea

**Context:** Solo founder considering a vertical SaaS tool for freelancers.

## Commands

1. **`/researcher`** — "Plan 5 customer interviews for freelancers who invoice manually. What demand signals prove pain vs nice-to-have?"
2. **`/product-manager`** — "From interview themes [paste], scope a 1-week MVP. Cut everything else."
3. **`/ceo-founder-coach`** — "Go/pivot/kill memo. Name the riskiest assumption for this week."

## Success

CEO coach returns a single weekly needle-mover with kill conditions.
